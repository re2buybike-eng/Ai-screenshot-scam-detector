The Problem

Scams Are Getting Harder to Spot.

Fake payment confirmations, edited receipts, investment offers and believable chats can look genuine at first glance.

💳

Fake Payment Screenshots

Fraudsters create fake transaction confirmations to convince victims that money has been transferred.

🧾

Edited Receipts

Amounts, dates, reference numbers and other visible details can be manipulated.

💼

Fake Offers

Job, investment, shopping and loan scams often use screenshots to appear legitimate.

⚠

Social Engineering

Urgency, fear and emotional pressure can make people act before verifying.

▣

QR & Payment Scams

Suspicious QR and payment requests can redirect victims toward fraudulent destinations.

💬

Fake Conversations

WhatsApp, SMS and social media conversations can be manipulated to create believable stories.
